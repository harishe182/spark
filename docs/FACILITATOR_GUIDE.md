# Facilitator Guide

A quick reference for running Spark sessions with a child. The app is
**facilitator-first**: you set everything up; the child only sees the friendly
game screens.

## Before the first session

- Guardian consent is handled **outside** the app (paper/digital), per the
  study protocol. The app shows a child-friendly assent/game intro each session.
- Ensure the tablet is charged and (for AI-Spark participants) on Wi-Fi.

## Creating a participant

1. Tap **Enter** on the Spark console.
2. Tap **New participant**.
3. Choose the **age group** (8–9 / 10–11).
4. Choose **Condition**:
   - **Auto** (recommended) — keeps the two arms balanced automatically.
   - **AI** or **Static** — only if assigning manually per your randomization.
5. Tap **Create**. The participant gets an ID like `P001`. **No names are
   stored** — record the ID↔child mapping on your secure paper/secure roster.

> The condition chip (AI-Spark / Static-Spark) is shown to **you** only. Never
> read it aloud or show the setup screens to the child.

## Running a session

1. Tap a participant → you'll see their condition and the session list.
2. (Optional) **Pilot mode** toggle compresses all timings for a quick dry-run.
   Leave it **off** for real sessions.
3. Pick the session:
   - **Pre-Test** — once, before week 1. No hints.
   - **Week 1–10** — the weekly creativity session (Spark appears).
   - **Post-Test** — once, after week 10. No hints.
4. The child sees **"Let's play!"** (assent) → tap to begin.

### Weekly session flow (~15 minutes)

| Phase | ~Time | What happens |
|-------|------|--------------|
| Intro | 1 min | Prompt shown; child taps "I'm ready!" |
| Ideas (divergent) | 6 min | Child types ideas; Spark offers a hint ~every 90s |
| Pick favorite | 1 min | Child taps their favorite idea |
| Drawing (convergent) | 6 min | Child draws; Spark hint ~every 2 min; snapshots auto-save |
| Reflection | 1 min | Three 1–5 smiley questions |

The **top bar** (facilitator-only) shows the phase, a precise countdown, a
**Next** button (advance early), and **■ End early**. Both the early-advance and
end-early actions are **logged** automatically.

### When Spark appears

The child sees a Spark card with the suggestion and **Accept** / **Reject**.
- Accept → the hint joins their idea list.
- Reject → it disappears.
Let the child decide. Don't steer.

### Finishing

After reflection, the child sees a celebration. You then get an optional
**engagement rating** and **notes** field. Tap **Save & finish**.

## If something goes wrong

- **Wi-Fi drops (AI-Spark):** the session continues; Spark simply shows fewer
  cards. No data is lost.
- **Need to stop early:** tap **■ End early** → confirm. Progress is saved and
  the session is marked *ended early* (logged).
- **App closed mid-session:** the session was saved as *in progress*; start a
  fresh session for that week and note it in your log.
